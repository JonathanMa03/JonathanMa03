## About me

I work at the intersection of applied statistics, optimization, and machine learning. My research asks how learning and decision-making systems can remain reliable under uncertainty, misspecification, and distribution shift (especially in bioinformatics and empirical economics!). I am especially interested in problems where the right methodology is not obvious, including cases where statistical reasoning, mathematical structure, and modern AI can complement one another, and I'm also an AuDHD-er and like to build projects for the sake of building!

### Research interests (Updated Nov. 2026)

Dynamic Factor Models, Discrete Choice Models, RLVR, Human-In-The-Loop Systems, Causal Discovery, Bayesian Data Borrowing and Information Transport, Genetic Optimization, Fair and Explainable AI
